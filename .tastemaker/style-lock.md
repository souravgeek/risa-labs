# CampusFest Growth Center
A compact operations desk for festival partnership teams. Cash collected anchors the first viewport. A dark green concert photograph and an acid-lime progress track connect the financial dashboard to its event.
Use DM Sans, tabular figures, forest #183e32, lime #d6f58a, ink #1c2824, muted #58665e, cool paper #f6f7f3 and white cards. Keep lime for fills with dark lettering. Use green for actionable evidence, amber for overdue and violet for in-kind. Use Lucide icons throughout. Honor reduced motion.
The navigation rail, snapshot date, workspace selector and copilot persist across views. Data figures open their supporting records. Small screens stack reading order and open navigation on demand.
Button, Badge and Card come from Tastemaker at commit 792a28a820ec4e9159ef8621c21a6c88528bbf2e. Scoped CSS applies this project's tokens.
